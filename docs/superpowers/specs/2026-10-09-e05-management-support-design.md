## Purpose, source facts and scope

John records a concrete project escalation and accountable owner. Adam or Victoria, with explicit project and department authority, assigns a helper for a permitted technical or BA constraint. An authorized recorder records the helper's explicit support, guidance or decline and proposes an evidence-linked result. A different authorized employee reviews that exact result. The queue distinguishes completed support, partial support and an unresolved problem, with missing or stale evidence visible. Ending a management action does not resolve the underlying task, QA handoff, defect or customer decision.

The selected approach is a small `Domain/Management` Laravel/PostgreSQL aggregate. Extending QA handoff into a universal action would conflate QA and customer-decision semantics; moving immediately to E06 would leave the management trace incomplete and introduce capacity/calendar assumptions. Existing `QaHandoffService` already implements `support_requested`, owner changes, acknowledgement and resolution. Existing BA records already implement request/answer/PM review and immutable scope obligations. Neither workflow is recreated or written by this module.

Existing foundations used as evidence are `docs/README.md`, `docs/contracts/qa-evidence-v1.md`, `qa-data-dictionary.md`, `ba-evidence-v1.md`, `ba-data-dictionary.md`, the approved E05-01/02 designs, `QaHandoffService`, `QaPins`, `BaContract`, `BaPins`, `BaCoordination`, `BaReadService`, `BaAuthority`, `DatabaseAccessGateway` and additive migrations 19–33. `CLAUDE.md` is absent. Verified role codes are `developer`, `qa`, `ba`, `pm`, `department_head`, `reviewer`; names such as John are synthetic fixture labels, never authorization rules.

## Identity and operation authority

Separate authenticated actor user, actor employee, escalation requester, accountable owner, assigned helper, support recorder, outcome reviewer and each event-time task assignee. Requester on action.open is the authenticated actor's employee, derived by the server. Owner/helper are explicitly selected employee UUIDs, never derived from names, aliases or current task assignee. One current helper and one current support fact per action episode keeps this slice independently reviewable; multiple helpers or independent problems use separate actions. A helper may equal the owner. Unknown helper is null only before assignment. Requester and non-null owner/helper must have exact current project memberships; captured event-time intervals remain separate.

All operations require trusted local bearer tenant/user, current same-project membership and explicitly scoped action purpose. Tenant/project never come from the body. The following **proposed v1 role/purpose matrix** is an engineering choice submitted for written review, not an already implemented permission or production policy. Existing generic `reviewer` and connector administrator do not inherit management powers.

## Exact references and read-only semantics

Each action version and each support fact has its own 1–20 unique references. A reference always contains `(task_version_id, criterion_id, criterion_version_id, relation)`; `relation` is `blocks` or `context`, plus discriminant and exact typed source subject below. A lifecycle-only reference has no additional subject. Other kinds are `qa_handoff_event`, `qa_defect_event`, `qa_execution_version`, `ba_request_version`, `ba_answer_version`, `ba_review`. UUIDs/hashes of exact objects are pinned; root IDs alone are insufficient. All context references are mandatory for privacy and source coverage, although they do not independently claim a blockage.

| Kind | Required semantic proof |
| --- | --- |
| lifecycle | Actual `task_criterion_links` tuple; exact `requirement_version_criteria` membership and requirement hash. Derive requirement version from the validated current approved requirement membership, never from a client hash. |
| qa_handoff_event | Exact event belongs to specified handoff; handoff task/criterion tuple equals reference. Its event execution/defect pins belong to that handoff's criterion/build/environment and defect lineage. Include original open receipt and selected event authority. |
| qa_defect_event | Exact event belongs to defect; its non-null task/criterion tuple equals reference. Bind definition/build/environment/execution/selection/fix/retest lineage and duplicate canonical provenance; unsupported or missing task relation is unknown, never a same-project substitute. |
| qa_execution_version | Exact series/attempt/version, definition and criterion; actual task-criterion tuple and captured execution responsibility match. Bind immutable build/environment, entry/definition-review/selection-review pins required by QA; current source head and attempt ancestry/retest pins determine applicability. |
| ba_request_version | Exact clarification/request version contains the identical request-link tuple and links hash. Include request subjects/coordinator and source party proof if present. |
| ba_answer_version | Exact answer pins that request/hash/links hash; referenced tuple is in that request; include answer source/party/predecessor provenance and recorded answer subject separately from projected head. |
| ba_review | Exact review binds the specified request/answer/link hashes; require actual tuple membership, PM proof and distinct employee rule; include immutable scope-obligation origin if present. |

Read adapters expand only allowlisted typed dependencies from those records. QA expansion includes exact source receipts, build/environment, definition/review, selected entry/plan/review, baseline/policy, series/attempt/version, defect event/fix/retest/duplicate and handoff provenance needed by the selected kind. BA expansion includes exact request/links, answer/predecessor, review, party/receipt/source and scope-obligation origin. Traversal never means arbitrary SQL table, URL, shell or user-specified join. Cycles, unsupported shapes, missing dependency rows or category overflow yield unknown; there is no favorable truncation. Shared subgraphs may be deduplicated by complete typed identity/hash, never by actor or UUID without type.

At creation/revision and support recording, reject a demonstrably inconsistent tuple/hash/lineage with conflict; retain valid historical but superseded source facts with stale applicability. Current favorable confirmation requires all mandatory action **and** support references current. QA current eligibility uses its original approved selection/entry/review pins and durable invalidation/current-head rules; BA current eligibility retains `scope_change_required` origin obligations and current request/answer/review rules. Management does not call QA/BA mutation, observation or maintenance methods. Read-only adapters implement the already documented deterministic checks under management coordination. A reviewed management `completed` outcome means the named support was completed, even when the source says QA blocked or BA scope approval pending; the DTO still declares `underlying_dependency_status=unresolved` or `unknown`. No adapter can declare underlying acceptance, readiness, scope clearance or release.

Support result references must overlap the action's task/criterion tuple set. Every support reference belongs to an action tuple, with the same criterion version; new post-support source evidence for that tuple is allowed and independently pinned. A changed task/criterion version requires explicit action.revise and fresh support. Unrelated same-project evidence cannot prove this action's outcome. Lifecycle, QA and BA references to the same tuple are permitted when their typed identities differ; all references to that tuple in a set must agree on relation. Duplicate `(kind,exact subject,tuple)` entries or contradictory tuple relations reject.

## States, revisions and honest outcomes

Business state: `open`, `assigned`, `awaiting_review`, `reviewed`, `withdrawn`. Separate `support_status=none|recorded|declined|retracted`, `review_status=unverified|confirmed|rejected|revoked`, `outcome=completed|partial|unresolved|null`, `applicability=current|stale|unknown|revoked`. `reviewed` is not synonymous with resolved: independently confirmed partial/unresolved outcomes are valid reviewed records. Decline/rejection/withdrawal never count as success.

| Event | Allowed state | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| action.open | creation | open, episode1, immutable owner/reason/source references, no helper/support/review |
| assignment.set | open/assigned/awaiting_review/reviewed | assigned; immutable owner/helper/department assignment boundary; invalidate current support/review; preserve all earlier facts |
| support.record | assigned | awaiting_review; one fact pins exact action/assignment versions and proposes completed/partial/unresolved |
| support.correct | awaiting_review/reviewed | awaiting_review; same source series/current support predecessor and new fact; no former confirmation carry |
| support.retract | awaiting_review/reviewed | assigned; exact current support predecessor retracted; no earlier-support fallback |
| support.decline | assigned | assigned with declined status and unresolved context; no reviewed completed/partial outcome; reassignment or fresh record may follow |
| outcome.confirm | awaiting_review | reviewed; exact fact/action/assignment/reference hashes and independent reviewer; all mandatory inputs current |
| outcome.reject | awaiting_review | assigned; rejected exact support retains proposed result/history, current outcome null; fresh support required |
| outcome.revoke | reviewed | awaiting_review with revoked review; exact predecessor review; fresh confirmation required |
| action.revise | any except withdrawn | open; new action version/episode with full owner/reason/reference set; helper cleared, old support/reviews historical |
| action.reopen | reviewed/withdrawn | open; new episode and full action version; explicit reason, no old favorable carry |
| action.withdraw | open/assigned/awaiting_review/reviewed | withdrawn with reason; no favorable readiness |
| context.add | any | unchanged; text only, no reference/state/proof replacement |

Revision/CAS failure, inapplicable operation or mismatched predecessor/hash is409. Assignment/revise/reopen/retraction/rejection invalidate the current support pointer as appropriate and reset current outcome; history still exposes its separately authorized exact subject fact. The event carries both `subject_support_id` (fact recorded/corrected/retracted/rejected) and projected `support_id`; they may differ for late facts or clearing transitions. Outcome revocation retains the current support fact with unverified outcome. Corrections and replacement assignments cannot make an earlier reviewer satisfy the new boundary.

Source ordering uses positive source_revision and exact predecessor, never arrival time. A late valid source revision below the current source head is retained as immutable historical subject and increments aggregate event revision but cannot replace the current support/outcome. A new head with missing predecessor or gap is unknown, retains its fact, clears favorable projection and requires fresh correctly linked evidence. Newer declined/retracted/unknown/rejected facts never fall back to earlier favorable support. Historical event state is its observed projection, not a rewritten claim of event-time current state.

Execution clarification2026-10-13: a support natural source series `(tenant,project,source_instance,support family,object_id)` binds to one exact action/action-version/assignment. A novel late revision with no predecessor must preserve that binding; another assignment uses a new series. Historical subject references and current projection references keep separate occurrence ownership and applicability. A valid current confirmed result is not cleared merely because an independently retained historical subject is stale; every mandatory historical privacy/original proof remains required for the acknowledgement.

Native BA terminal invalidity is a read-only source dependency, matched by tenant/project, typed subject and the hash of its stored native JSONB authority proof. A party uses its own registration receipt's proof. Restoration of the same grant cannot revive a terminal source fact. Terminal rows and captured absence are included in the bounded metadata closure/recheck; wrong type or proof hash cannot invalidate an unrelated subject. Management persists only its own observations and never invokes native BA observers or writers.

## Transaction, transport and replay

`ManagementCoordination` owns one tenant-scoped transaction: project row lock → lifecycle project state → management state/root/source heads in sorted UUID order → captured QA/BA/E03 mutable heads in fixed table order → current/original access rows in the established access lock order. It captures the bounded graph before closure, then locks/reloads and compares all captured heads and hashes. No nested QA/BA coordination, network call or write adapter runs inside it. Final PostgreSQL `clock_timestamp()` checks every required interval after callback and before commit; expiry/changed head rolls back. Reads use the same authority/clock fence before constructing the delivered DTO. Existing project serialization protects E03/QA/BA writers; read-only source locks provide exact head stability. Recheck every supported source head not protected by that lock explicitly; missing synchronization yields unknown.

Receipt, fact, event, head/generation and safe audit IDs/hash/reason commit atomically before success. Transaction failure returns no successful fact. Current/original authority and terminal observations precede exact replay or alias binding. Replay returns the same recorded output fact, with freshly evaluated current applicability; it never returns a saved favorable DTO. Same natural/request identity with a different canonical hash409; exact authorized replay200 without duplicate fact/event/audit. Natural identity is `(tenant,project,source_instance,family,object_id,source_revision)`, request identity `(tenant,project,requester,operation,key)`. Exact canonical SHA256 recursively sorts object keys and normalized reference sets while retaining meaningful array order. Expected counters are controls, excluded from content hash; source identity fields and typed content are included. No end-to-end exactly-once claim.

## Current/original authorization and applicability

Three checks remain independent: caller's present ability to see/act, each named person's present permitted scope, and original authority of mandatory action/assignment/support/review/source facts. Exact saved membership/role/grant/policy/connector IDs, revisions and `[valid_from,valid_to)` intervals must still match and be effective at the final clock. Replacement rows cannot repair old proof. Current viewer denial only withholds; it is not proof that the original actor was revoked. Unsupported proof shape/missing ordering or coverage is unknown, never invented revocation.

Execution clarification2026-10-09: the eight named slots per boundary/reference describe direct explicit named requests. Nested saved-proof named/coordinator/request-subject pins are additionally charged once per original proof slot into the unchanged total named allowance `min(8B+8L,2000)` before lookup deduplication. They are validated by the strict saved-format authority validator and are not duplicated as explicit named requests. All mandatory receipt/event/fact originals remain. A simple BA reference may therefore consume nine total named charges while having three explicit named slots; larger source graphs may honestly exceed the total allowance. No numeric cap or privacy check is relaxed.

Proven revocation/expiry or source invalidity is observed monotonically in management-local terminal records keyed to the exact mandatory proof. Restore/regrant does not revive observed invalidity. Wholly unobserved same-row revoke/restore cannot be reconstructed by the existing access store; disclose that inherited limitation rather than claiming historical access-ledger support. Source correction, new current task/criterion/requirement, changed selection or BA obligation makes affected current applicability stale/unknown even if history says confirmed. No broad project invalidation of unaffected references. Synchronous read/review/replay evaluation is authoritative; no stale cached favorable response and no source invalidation callback.

Fresh action/assignment/support/review boundaries can establish new authority without rewriting history. Optional older history facts are independently filtered/withheld and do not invalidate an otherwise fresh current record. Conversely, mandatory origin proofs, including QA selection and BA scope obligation, cannot be discarded as optional to obtain a favorable projection. Withdraw/relink/new management action does not clear the original BA obligation or QA state. Only the separate source workflow can change those states.
